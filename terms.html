Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

1. Custom Manufacturing & Orders

High Peak Sports specializes in private-label manufacturing, custom PVC patches, goalkeeper gloves, and sportswear. All bulk production begins only after sample or artwork sign-off from the client.

2. Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs)

MOQs apply depending on the product type (e.g., typically 25+ pieces for custom patches). Details are specified during direct quote inquiries.

3. Production Timelines

Standard production runs take between 7 to 14 working days following final design approval, excluding international transit times.

Clients warrant that all logos, artwork, and brand assets provided for custom manufacturing do not infringe upon any third-party copyrights or trademarks.
