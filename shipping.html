Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Worldwide Delivery

High Peak Sports dispatches manufactured goods directly from Sialkot, Pakistan, to international destinations worldwide via trusted courier services (such as DHL, FedEx, and UPS) or air/sea cargo for massive bulk orders.

2. Dispatch Windows

Once your custom production run is complete and quality checked, orders are packed and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Tracking details are provided via email.

3. Custom Duties and Import Taxes

International clients are responsible for any local import duties, custom clearances, or taxes levied by their respective destination countries.
