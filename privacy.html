Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit a manufacturing inquiry, we collect information such as your name, email address, company name, and project specifications (including custom designs or artwork files).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to communicate with you regarding your manufacturing orders, provide quotes, process samples, and manage shipments worldwide from Sialkot, Pakistan.

3. Data Security

Your custom designs, product specs, and personal information are kept strictly confidential. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside third parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out to us directly at info@highpeaksports.us.
