Custom PVC Patch Specifications & Options

Material Options

  • Soft PVC (Rubber): Flexible, durable, ideal for 2D and 3D designs
  • Hard PVC: Rigid, premium feel, best for flat 2D designs
  • Eco-friendly PVC: Phthalate-free, REACH/RoHS compliant options

Design Capabilities

  • 2D Flat Patches: Single or multi-layer, crisp detail reproduction
  • 3D Sculpted Patches: Multi-layer with raised elements for depth
  • Custom Shapes: Die-cut to any outline - no shape restrictions
  • Color Matching: Pantone matching, unlimited colors per design
  • Gradients & Halftones: Photo-realistic reproduction available

Backing Options

  • Hook-and-Loop (Velcro): Standard for tactical & uniform use
  • Sew-On: Permanent attachment for apparel
  • Iron-On / Heat Seal: Quick application for fabrics
  • Adhesive / Peel-Stick: Temporary or permanent mounting
  • Pin Back: For collectible or display patches
  • Magnetic: For metal surfaces

Size & Thickness

  • Minimum Size: 1 inch (25mm) minimum dimension
  • Maximum Size: 12 inches (300mm) - larger by request
  • Standard Thickness: 2mm, 3mm, 4mm, 5mm
  • 3D Sculpted Depth: Up to 5mm raised elements
  • Custom Thickness: Available per specification

How We Manufacture Custom PVC Patches

1. Design Review & Artwork Prep

Our design team reviews your artwork (AI, EPS, PDF, high-res PNG) and provides a digital proof with Pantone colors, layer structure, and mold layout within 24 hours.

2. Mold Creation

CNC-machined metal molds are created from your approved design. Multi-layer molds for 3D patches require separate cavities per layer. Mold cost included in MOQ.

3. PVC Injection & Curing

Liquid PVC is injected into heated molds under pressure. Each color layer is injected and cured sequentially. 3D patches require precise layer alignment.

4. Backing Application & QC

Hook-and-loop, adhesive, or sew-on backing applied. Every patch inspected for color accuracy, mold fill, edge quality, and backing adhesion before packing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Our typical MOQ is 25–30 pieces for custom PVC patches. Complex designs or 3D sculpted patches may require 50+ pieces. Contact us for specific requirements.

Do you offer 2D and 3D options?

Yes. 2D patches are flat with layered colors. 3D patches have sculpted raised elements for depth and tactile feel. Both support unlimited colors.

What backing options do you offer?

Hook-and-loop (Velcro), sew-on, iron-on, adhesive, pin back, and magnetic. Hook-and-loop is standard for tactical/uniform use.

Can you match Pantone colors?

Yes. We provide custom Pantone color matching for all PVC patch orders. Provide Pantone codes with your artwork for exact matching.

What is the production timeline?

Standard production: 7–14 working days after artwork approval. Rush orders available (5–7 days) for additional fee. Shipping: 3–7 days express worldwide.

Can you ship worldwide from Pakistan?

Yes. We ship globally via DHL, FedEx, UPS, and air/sea cargo from Sialkot. Tracking provided. Client responsible for import duties.

What file formats do you accept?

Preferred: AI, EPS, PDF (vector). Also accept: PSD, high-res PNG (300+ DPI). We provide digital proof before production.

Do you provide pre-production samples?

Yes. Digital proofs included free. Physical pre-production samples available (cost credited toward bulk order). Sample production: 5–7 days.
